Companies

Companies that do the kind of work your CV shows - including the ones that hire without posting on any job board. Swiss companies come from the commercial register, found by their registered purpose; Austrian and Benelux ones from OpenStreetMap's offices, read off their own homepage. Each is ranked against your CV; for the best matches the app reads their careers page, adds any openings to your Jobs list, and lets you approach the rest directly.
